# Xiaohang Li

**Robotics · Control · Robot Learning**

I'm a B.Eng. student in Electronic and Computer Engineering (EE track) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen (CUHK-Shenzhen). I build and evaluate robot manipulation and control systems, from language-guided grasping on a physical arm to simulation-based control research.

My current interests are **model-based control, robot learning, and physical human–robot interaction**. My research includes adaptive model predictive control (MPC) at iHEAR Lab and learning and whole-body control for modular robots at Freeform Robotics Lab.

[Website](https://block-o2.github.io/) · [Research CV](https://block-o2.github.io/Xiaohang_Li_CV.pdf?v=20261104)

## Selected projects

- **[Sagittarius Semantic Grasp](https://github.com/Block-O2/sagittarius-semantic-grasp)** — Language-guided pick-and-place on a physical Sagittarius arm, integrating GroundingDINO, ROS1, MoveIt, camera calibration, and confidence-gated execution.
- **[MT3 Dynamic Alignment](https://github.com/Block-O2/MT3-Dynamic-Alignment)** — Moving-object trajectory transfer through Kalman filtering, latency compensation, and object-relative demonstration replay in PyBullet, without policy retraining. Hardware validation remains future work.
- **[Semantic Manipulation Stack](https://github.com/Block-O2/semantic-manipulation-stack)** — Ongoing exploration of structured task plans, bounded Pick/Place/Push skills, local recovery, and outcome checks in MuJoCo/robosuite. Current evidence is limited to a fixed simulated workspace.
- **[NavRL Replication](https://github.com/Block-O2/NavRL_replication)** — Reproduction work in Isaac Sim, checkpoint comparisons, and ROS integration preparation, with simulation/offline evidence and no real-flight validation.
- **[Personal Website](https://github.com/Block-O2/Block-O2.github.io)** — Research background, project demonstrations, and my one-page research CV.

**Ongoing control research:** [adaptive-traction-mpc](https://github.com/Block-O2/adaptive-traction-mpc) — Simulation-based engineering and research on adaptive MPC for robot-assisted lower-limb rehabilitation at iHEAR Lab. Work in progress; no hardware or clinical validation.
